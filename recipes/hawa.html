Hawawshi

Hawawshi is a traditional Egyptian street food classic consisting of seasoned minced meat—typically beef or lamb—stuffed directly into a pocket of flatbread or fresh yeast dough before cooking. The raw meat mixture is mixed with finely diced onions, peppers, fresh herbs, and a fragrant spice blend featuring cumin, coriander, allspice, and paprika. Once filled, the flatbread is brushed with oil or clarified butter and baked or grilled until the crust turns golden and crunchy, while the inside cooks into a juicy, tender meat patty. Served scorching hot alongside pickled vegetables, tahini sauce, or a fresh salad, Hawawshi is a savory, comforting staple of everyday Egyptian cuisine.
